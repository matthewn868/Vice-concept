About VICE

Port of Spain's
Premier Night Experience

VICE Nightclub & Lounge is Trinidad's answer to world-class nightlife. Born from a desire to bring an authentic club experience back to Port of Spain, VICE fuses high energy music, top-tier DJs, and an upscale atmosphere unlike anything else in the Caribbean.

Whether you're here for the soca, the R&B, or the Latin vibes — VICE delivers unforgettable nights every single weekend.
